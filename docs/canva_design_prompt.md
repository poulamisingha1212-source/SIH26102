# Canva Design Prompts — MPLADS AI Sentinel

Copy-paste these into Canva AI (Magic Design) to generate better designs for the website.

## 1. Main Prompt — Website / Dashboard Design

> Design a modern, professional government web dashboard called "MPLADS AI Sentinel" — an AI-powered audit and fraud-risk prioritization platform for India's Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), monitoring public funds under the MP Local Area Development Scheme.
>
> **Style:** Swiss minimalism meets civic tech — clean, trustworthy, data-dense but airy. Soft light theme with white rounded cards (12px radius, subtle shadows) on a very light slate background. Generous whitespace, thin slate borders, clear visual hierarchy.
>
> **Colors:** Background #F8FAFC, cards #FFFFFF, text #1E293B, primary indigo #4F46E5, light indigo accent #EEF2FF, borders #E2E8F0. Risk-tier colors: red #DC2626 (High Risk), amber (Medium Risk), emerald green (Low Risk). Chart palette: indigo, sky blue, emerald, amber, rose.
>
> **Typography:** Outfit for headings (bold, geometric), Work Sans for body text, monospace Fira Code for numbers and data tables.
>
> **Sections to include:**
> 1. Header with a shield logo, product name "MPLADS AI Sentinel", a green "Data Live" status pill, and a role selector
> 2. KPI stat row: Total Funds Sanctioned, Works Monitored, High-Risk Flags, Avg Utilization — with animated number counters
> 3. "Priority Queue" — a ranked audit case list with risk-tier badges (High Risk – Review, Medium Risk – Monitor, Low Risk) and priority scores
> 4. Portfolio Overview with a doughnut chart (execution status) and horizontal bar chart (fund share by category), plus a state/MP risk ranking list
> 5. MP Fund Directory — searchable public table of MPs with sanctioned amount, utilization %, and risk score
> 6. Footer with MoSPI branding and "Explainable AI — decision support only, human review required" disclaimer
>
> **Vibe:** Government-grade trust, transparency, modern civic technology like Data.gov or the UK GOV.UK design system, but more polished and modern. Include subtle dot-pattern background texture and one hero image of an Indian government building or civic infrastructure. WCAG accessible, high contrast.

## Tips for using these in Canva

1. **Where to paste:** Canva → Create a design → **Website** (or Canva AI / Magic Design prompt box) → paste the main prompt.
2. **Generate in parts:** Canva's AI handles shorter instructions better. If the full prompt overloads it, generate section-by-section (e.g., just "KPI stat cards row" or "risk badge list row") and assemble manually.
3. **Lock the palette first:** Create a Canva Brand Kit with #4F46E5, #1E293B, #F8FAFC, #EEF2FF, #DC2626 and the Outfit / Work Sans fonts so every generation stays on-theme.
4. **Iterate with follow-ups:** After generation, refine with short follow-ups like "make the cards more rounded", "reduce chart clutter to 6 slices", "make risk badges softer tints instead of solid red".
5. **Use it for assets, not just pages:** The same prompts work for Canva presentations — great for the SIH26102 pitch deck using the landing-page prompt above.

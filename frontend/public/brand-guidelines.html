Jannidhi Portal Identity Redesign

Modernized visual identity for the national public fund governance portal. Preserving the iconic civic triad—the 3-citizen arch ("Jan"), the radiant solar dawn, the public treasury vessel ("Nidhi") with ₹ glyph, and the sustainable development sprout—re-engineered into razor-sharp, scalable vector architecture.

Design Evolution & Redesign Concepts

Concept 1 • SELECTED & POLISHED

The Sovereign Civic Arch

Direct, surgical modernization of the original identity. Replaces muddy multi-color outlines with golden-ratio geometric curves. Integrates the 3-citizen unity dome with daylight transparency rays and an embossed rupee treasury bowl.

Strongest Concept (Selected)
Concept 2 • ALTERNATIVE

The Digital Governance Gateway

A tech-forward, high-security geometric shield emblem. Features digital nodes (representing citizen assemblies & monitoring nodes) linking directly into an open portal gateway and data archway.

Tech-Heavy Variation
Concept 3 • ALTERNATIVE

The Continuous Civic Ribbon

An infinity-style dynamic growth ribbon intertwining citizen trust and public fund growth. Expresses seamless money-flow tracking, real-time audit transparency, and upward developmental trajectory.

Fluid Growth Variation
